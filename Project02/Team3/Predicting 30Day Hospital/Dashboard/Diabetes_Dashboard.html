    ⚠ Subgroup Fairness Alert — Age

    False-negative-rate (FNR) gap across subgroups · test set · tuned thresholds

    • Age shows a severe disparity in missed readmissions in all four models (FNR gap 0.885–0.967); in every model at least one age band has FNR = 1.00, i.e. no true early readmission is caught.
    • Race gaps are moderate (0.083–0.288, largest for Logistic Regression) and gender gaps are small (0.006–0.031).
    • Evidence note: the zero-recall bands are the youngest ones, with only 1 positive in [0–10) and 6 in [10–20) in the test set, so the exact size of the gap is uncertain — but the model should not be applied to patients under 20 without separate validation.
